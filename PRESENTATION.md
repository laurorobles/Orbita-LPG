# 🌟 PRESENTATION — ORBITA-LPG

**Orbita-LPG** is a **free and open-source (GPLv3)** 6-voice West Coast generative synthesizer with an integrated Euclidean sequencer. Inspired by Don Buchla's modular philosophy, it unifies polymetric rhythm and non-linear timbre in one immediate instrument.

## What Makes It Unique?

- **Rhythm + Timbre Unified:** Six independent Euclidean rings drive six West Coast voices directly—rhythm shapes timbre, and timbre shapes rhythm.
- **Effortless Polymetry:** Independent cycle lengths (2–32 steps), rotations, clock dividers (`1/4` to `1/32`), and real groove swing generate constantly evolving patterns.
- **Authentic West Coast Synthesis:** Morphing oscillators, 1st-order ADAA Wavefolding, Auto-FM feedback, Pitch-Drop transients, and optical Vactrol Low Pass Gates.
- **Stereo RE-201 Space Echo:** Tape delay with quadrature Wow/Flutter, warm feedback filtering, and smooth pitch-glide modulation.
- **100% Free & Open Source:** Full source code on GitHub under GNU GPLv3, with free pre-built binaries for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
